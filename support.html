Common questions

  • Restore purchases: Open the Shop screen and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same App Store account used for the original purchase (remove ads, coin packs, neon theme).
  • Ads still showing after remove ads: Restore purchases, then restart the app. If the issue continues, email us with your device model and iOS version.
  • Progress: Stack Rush saves progress locally on your device. Uninstalling the app may erase local progress.
  • Bug reports: Include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
