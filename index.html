Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how John Shugart ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Stack Rush (App Store Connect listing name: Stack Rush: Block Tower; bundle ID com.johnshugart.stackrush) (the "Game"). By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

01 Information We Collect

The Game does not require a traditional account with an email address or password. Stack Rush is primarily an offline game; progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Depending on how you play, we or our service partners may collect or process the following:

  • Local game data: scores, coins, unlocks, settings, and related progress stored on your device.
  • Purchase information: when you buy in-app products (for example, remove ads, coin packs, or the neon theme), purchase receipts or tokens are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us for help, we receive the information you choose to include in that message.
We do not collect your real name, postal address, or precise location for gameplay. The Game does not require you to create an account.

02 How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Operate core game features and save progress locally on your device
  • Validate in-app purchases and unlock paid content
  • Improve performance and stability
  • Respond to support requests

03 Advertising

Stack Rush does not show third-party advertisements. The Game does not use Google AdMob or another ad network, does not initialize an advertising SDK, and does not request your advertising identifier (IDFA) for ads.

Stack Rush does not use third-party advertising.

04 In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases include coin packs and a neon theme. A remove-ads product may still be restored through Apple for earlier purchases; the current Game does not show third-party ads. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. Apple’s privacy practices apply to payment processing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the corresponding content; we do not store your payment card number.

05 Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party service:

  • Apple App Store: in-app purchase processing and receipt validation.

Apple processes purchase data according to its own privacy policy. We share only what is necessary to complete purchases and operate the Game.

06 Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience consistent with its age rating on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

07 Data Retention & Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing app data typically removes local progress. Purchase data retained by Apple is governed by Apple’s policies. You may contact us at the email below with privacy or deletion questions related to information we hold (for example, support emails).

08 Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate for a primarily offline mobile game. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Apple applies its own security practices to purchase data it processes.

09 Your Choices

  • You may play without providing an email address.
  • You may reset or clear local progress by deleting and reinstalling the Game (or clearing app data where available).
  • In-app purchases are optional and processed by Apple under Apple’s terms.
